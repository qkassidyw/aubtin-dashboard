🔴 Root Cause Analysis

  • Single biggest gap: Aubtin ends 9 of 11 calls without asking for money. The deposit ask rate is 18.2% — not because he can't close, but because 34.5 LBs/call of hedge language creates an environment where asking feels presumptuous.
  • The LB-deposit connection: Calls with >30 LBs have near-zero deposit ask rate. The verbal hedging is a leading indicator — by close time, Aubtin has trained himself to expect a soft exit.
  • ICP is NOT the problem: Average ICP score is 8.2/10. The leads are excellent. The gap is entirely in what happens in the final 5 minutes of the call.

4-Week Coaching Sprint

Week 1: The Non-Negotiable Deposit Ask

Every single call ends with: "To lock in your spot, we take a $5,000 deposit today. I'll send the link right after." Practice it 50x before Monday. No variations, no softening. State it flat and hold 8 seconds of silence.

Week 2: Eliminate Hedge Language

Remove "kind of", "sort of", "I think", "basically", "I guess", "maybe" from every sentence. Replace with declarative statements. Record every call — each hedge is a coaching rep. Target: fewer than 5 hedges per call.

Week 3: The Kind Warrior Objection Sequence

For every objection: (1) Reframe as ROI question, (2) Quantify cost of inaction, (3) Ask for the deposit. Stopping at step 1 = Adequate. Completing all 3 = Strong. Review recordings with Hunter and flag every stopped sequence.

Week 4: Talk Ratio Discipline

After every prospect sentence, ask ONE question before speaking again. Target: prospect talks 55%+ of the call. Use the 2-second pause before responding — the discomfort is discovery happening in real time.
