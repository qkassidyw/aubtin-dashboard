🏆 Root Cause

Aubtin books excellent prospects and builds solid rapport. His #1 gap: he does not ask for the deposit. 0.0% ask rate across 12 calls. The Kind Warrior standard requires asking every single time — confidently, not apologetically.

Priority 1 — Deposit Ask

Ask for the $5K deposit on every call. Script: "To lock in your spot, we take a $5,000 deposit today." Then hold silence.

Priority 2 — Kill Weak Qualifiers

Avg 10.4 LBs/call. Replace "kind of", "I think", "maybe" with direct statements. Say "This will save you $X" — not "This might help".

Priority 3 — Urgency Creation

Only 0 of 12 calls had urgency language. Add: "We onboard a limited number of clients each month — I have one spot this week."
