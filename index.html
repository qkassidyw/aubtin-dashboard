Objection Playbook — Kind Warrior Scripts

Price too high:
"I understand — let me ask: if you could get [prospect's pain] solved in 30 days, what would that be worth? [silence] That's exactly why our clients see 10x ROI. The deposit is $5,000 today."
Need to think about it:
"Absolutely — what specifically do you need to think through? [listen] Great. Based on what you told me about [pain], the cost of not acting is [X]. Can we get started today?"
Need partner approval:
"Of course — if YOU were the sole decision-maker, would you move forward? [yes] Perfect. Let's get your partner on a quick call this week. I'll hold your spot with a $5K deposit."
Not the right time:
"When IS the right time? [answer] So in [X months], this problem will still exist — just bigger. The businesses I work with said the same thing 6 months ago. The deposit locks in today's price."
